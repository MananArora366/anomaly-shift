ANOMALY SHIFT

Work as a night security guard at a research facility booth from 23:00 to 07:00. Inspect visitor credentials, cross-reference logbooks, and detect impostors before they enter.

HOW TO PLAY

Read the directive at the top of each shift. Compare the visitor's badge with the logbook, and use the desk phone if you need more information.

Press A or AUTHORIZE ENTRY for a valid visitor. Press SPACE or EMERGENCY ALARM for an anomaly. Each correct decision earns 500 points. Three mistakes end the run.

Read the result before the next visitor arrives. Complete all three shifts to finish. Register and log in to save your best score; guest scores last only for the session.

1. Credential Verification

Inspect visitor ID credentials on your security desk. Verify employee names, clearance levels, and expiration dates against facility records.

2. Logbook Cross-Referencing

Check the daily visitor registry. If an unlisted person approaches, use your desk phone to call internal extensions.

3. 3-Strike Security Protocol

Authorize genuine employees or sound the alarm on fake anomalies. 3 mistakes result in an instant facility breach.
